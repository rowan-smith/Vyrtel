# Vyrtel Corporate Contributor License Agreement

**Version 1.0**

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") lets a company or other organisation
authorise its employees and contractors to contribute to the Vyrtel project, and grants the project
rights in those Contributions. It is for organisations that own, or may own, intellectual property
created by the people who contribute on their behalf.

Each Authorized Contributor also signs the [Individual Contributor License Agreement](individual.md)
(the CLA bot asks for it on their first pull request). This Agreement covers the organisation's
rights; the individual agreement covers the person's.

## How to sign

1. Fill in the details and Schedule A below, and have someone authorised to bind the organisation
   sign it.
2. Email the signed copy (PDF is fine) to **cla@vyrtel.com**.
3. The Project Owner confirms by reply. Until then, Contributions from the people listed may be held.

To add or remove Authorized Contributors later, have the Point of Contact email an updated Schedule A
to the same address.
